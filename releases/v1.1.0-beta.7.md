# Ramu v1.1.0-beta.7

## Changed

- **A turn you stop reads as stopped, not failed.** After **Stop**, the transcript says **Stopped after …** with no **Agent failed** row, the agent stays ready for your next message, and the thread lists show the thread as **Stopped** instead of **Failed**, on the desktop and the phone. A turn that fails on its own still shows as failed.
- **Dragging a folder onto the project list** shows the same drop overlay as files dragged into a chat, saying **Drop to add as a project**.
- In the desktop sidebar, a thread's status mark sits under its project's folder icon, so every title starts at the same edge and the age stays at the right.

## Fixed

- Opening a thread on the phone lands at its end, even when its newest rows take a moment to lay out.
- The phone's thread list no longer marks every thread **Offline** while it is still connecting to its host after the app opens; only a host that can't be reached does.
- **Drop to add to the message** over a chat is translated.

## Downloads

Choose the installer matching your OS and CPU: macOS arm64 (Apple Silicon), macOS x64 (Intel), or Linux x64 or arm64 (.deb, .rpm, AppImage or ZIP). SHA256SUMS files accompany the downloads.

## Known limitations

macOS builds update themselves; Linux installs outside a package manager update by hand. Windows releases are not available yet. Agent providers require their own installation and authentication.
