# Ramu v0.1.0-alpha.9

## Changed

- Agents: start a message with `@` and a model (such as `@opus-5.5`), one of your custom agents (such as `@security-reviewer`) or an ability (`@images`, `@search`, `@long`) to ask the main agent for that helper. It starts the helper with your request, read-only unless you ask for changes, and passes on its report. The helper's card works like any other: open it to read, message or stop it.
- Agents: every helper's card shows what it changed (files and lines), its turns and its reported cost.

## Removed

- Teammates: the **To** recipient chip, Review and Take over, **Add teammate…** and `/teammate`, agent-suggested teammates and their setting, the pause after four rounds, and **Save as teammate**. Ask for a helper by name instead; custom agents keep reusable reviewers.

## Downloads

Choose the installer matching your OS and CPU: macOS arm64 (Apple Silicon), macOS x64 (Intel), or Linux x64 or arm64 (.deb, .rpm, AppImage or ZIP). SHA256SUMS files accompany the downloads.

## Known limitations

Desktop alpha. macOS builds update themselves; Linux installs outside a package manager update by hand. Windows releases are not available yet. Agent providers require their own installation and authentication.
