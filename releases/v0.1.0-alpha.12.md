# Ramu v0.1.0-alpha.12

## Added

- Ramu Link: a free address for reaching a host from anywhere, such as `ramu-3f9c2a1b0d.akalstack.com`, that stays the same between restarts. Sign in to your Ramu account, then choose **Settings → Remote access → From anywhere → Ramu Link**. Each account has one link, for one computer, with up to 5 devices connected through it at a time and 10 GB a month.
- Ramu Link keeps your address while you use it. An address that no device reaches for 28 days is released so someone else can use the slot, with a warning a week before. **Give the slot back** releases it straight away.
- When every Ramu Link slot is taken, saving puts the computer on the waitlist; it switches on by itself when a slot frees. Meanwhile, Settings recommends what this computer can use instead: Tailscale, your own relay, a quick tunnel or ngrok.
- Your own Ramu Link relay: run the same relay on your free Cloudflare account and enter its address under **Your own relay**, with no slot to wait for. See [Run your own](https://github.com/akalstack/ramu/tree/main/apps/link#run-your-own).
- CLI: `ramu tunnel link [relay]` turns on Ramu Link, and `ramu tunnel release` gives the slot back.
- Phone app: a first-run setup, one step per screen: connect to your computer, choose how it is reached (with Ramu Link for away from home) and turn on notifications. A reopened app picks up where you left off, and **Skip** lets you look around first.
- Release notes end with the people who contributed to the release.

## Fixed

- Desktop setup: choosing **From a backup** or **From another host** under **Restore your settings?** no longer skips setup if the app closes before the restore is applied.

## Downloads

Choose the installer matching your OS and CPU: macOS arm64 (Apple Silicon), macOS x64 (Intel), or Linux x64 or arm64 (.deb, .rpm, AppImage or ZIP). SHA256SUMS files accompany the downloads.

## Known limitations

Desktop alpha. macOS builds update themselves; Linux installs outside a package manager update by hand. Windows releases are not available yet. Agent providers require their own installation and authentication. Ramu Link traffic passes through Cloudflare; every call still needs a paired device.
