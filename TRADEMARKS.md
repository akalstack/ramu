- Use, change and share the code under its license.
- Run your own Ramu Link for yourself, your team or your organization, and call it Ramu Link inside that group.
- Share unmodified builds of Ramu under the name, with the logo.
- Say truthfully that your project is "based on Ramu", "a fork of Ramu" or "works with Ramu".
- Use the name and logo in articles, reviews, tutorials and talks about Ramu.

## You need a different name and logo to

- Publish a modified version of the app, or offer a modified version of Ramu Link as a service.
- Offer any Ramu Link service to the public, free or paid.
- Name a product, company, domain or account after Ramu, or suggest the Ramu project made or endorses your work.

When you rename a fork, change the visible app name, the icon, and the service name in the app and on the service's pages.
